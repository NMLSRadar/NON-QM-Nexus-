The glossary assumes you know the mortgage business — it explains what NEXUS
does with each term, not what the term means. Each entry links to the feature
where you will meet it.

<GlossaryTerm id="1099-income" name="1099 income">
Income documented through 1099 forms instead of W-2s. NEXUS treats it as its
own income-documentation type (`1099` in the matrix&apos;s Doc types column) and
only ranks programs whose guidelines accept 1099-based income.
[Used in: Programs](#programs)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="asset-depletion" name="Asset depletion">
Qualifying from liquid assets drawn down over the loan term rather than earned
income. NEXUS models it as an income-documentation type; programs that accept
it carry an `asset_depletion` Doc type, and the Unique Non-QM Products tag often
applies.
[Used in: Manual Scenario](#manual-scenario) · [Unique Non-QM Products](#unique-nonqm-products)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="bank-statement-income" name="Bank statement income">
Non-QM&apos;s most common self-employed income proof: qualifying from deposits or
net income across 12 or 24 months of personal and/or business statements. In
NEXUS you can say it as a vital, and the form expands with which months and
account types apply. Lenders differ on what counts as income — the match cards
surface **Bank Statement Flexibility** and **Clean-File Execution** where a
lender explicitly addresses it.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Lender List](#lender-list)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="cltv" name="CLTV">
Combined loan-to-value: all liens against the property, not just the first. It
matters whenever a scenario has a second lien or HELOC behind the first
mortgage — NEXUS checks the program&apos;s combined-lien limits, not just the
first-lien LTV.
[Used in: Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="dscr" name="DSCR">
Debt-service coverage ratio — property income over the proposed payment. In
NEXUS it is both a vital and a program dimension: say “DSCR” as the income
documentation, and programs quote a minimum DSCR (`DSCR ≥ 1.0`) that the
scenario&apos;s rental income is checked against.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Programs](#programs)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="expense-factor" name="Expense factor">
The percentage of rental income a lender deducts (typically 25%) when
calculating a DSCR borrower&apos;s net rental income, or the fixed propensity used
for business-expense assumptions on bank-statement files. When a program&apos;s
guideline states one, NEXUS applies that exact factor to the scenario&apos;s income
before checking ratios.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="first-time-investor" name="First-time investor">
A borrower who owns no prior investment property. NEXUS models this as a
separate dimension from first-time homebuyer and uses it to filter investor-
experience requirements — some DSCR programs explicitly exclude first-time
investors or cap their LTV.
[Used in: Manual Scenario](#manual-scenario) · [Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="foreign-national" name="Foreign national">
A non-U.S. borrower without permanent-resident status, often with no U.S. FICO.
NEXUS treats it as a citizenship vital and only ranks programs whose verified
guidelines admit foreign nationals — the **Foreign National** and **Foreign
National Specialist** pills mark them.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Unique Non-QM Products](#unique-nonqm-products)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="full-doc" name="Full doc">
Traditional documentation: W-2s, tax returns, paystubs. NEXUS models it as the
`full_doc` income type and uses it as the baseline most borrowers think of —
the programs matrix marks which lenders still take it in the NON-QM lane.
[Used in: Programs](#programs) · [Documentation Needs](#documentation-needs)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="interest-only" name="Interest-only">
A mortgage where early payments cover interest only, lowering the qualifying
payment. NEXUS captures it as a requested feature (`interest_only_requested`),
and result cards pill programs where **Interest-Only** is available — which
materially changes DSCR and DTI math.
[Used in: Manual Scenario](#manual-scenario) · [Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="itin" name="ITIN">
Individual Taxpayer Identification Number — a U.S. tax ID for borrowers who
cannot get an SSN. A citizenship vital in NEXUS: say “ITIN” and only programs
whose current guidelines list ITIN eligibility are ranked; ITIN-specialist
lenders carry a dedicated pill.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Unique Non-QM Products](#unique-nonqm-products)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="ltv" name="LTV">
Loan-to-value: loan amount over property value. A core vital — provide it
directly or let NEXUS derive it from value and loan amount. Every program row
in the matrix has a max LTV; it is usually the first filter that bites.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Programs](#programs)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="non-permanent-resident" name="Non-permanent resident">
A visa holder (e.g. E-2, H-1B, L-1) who lives in the U.S. without a green card.
NEXUS treats the visa type as part of the citizenship vital — an F-1 holder is
classified as a foreign national, while work-visa residents are matched against
programs that accept non-permanent residency.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Manual Scenario](#manual-scenario)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="non-warrantable-condo" name="Non-warrantable condo">
A condo that fails Fannie/Freddie warrantability (investor concentration,
litigation, limited commercial space, etc.). NEXUS models it as a distinct
property type (`non_warrantable_condo`) with its own LTV cap tier and only
ranks programs that underwrite it as such.
[Used in: Voice Scenario](#voice-scenario) · [Programs](#programs)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="pnl-only" name="P&amp;L only">
A 12-month profit-and-loss statement as the sole income document — no tax
returns required on P&L-only programs. In NEXUS it is the `pnl_only` income
type; CPA attestation is confirmation of filing where a program asks for it,
never the income proof itself.
[Used in: Programs](#programs) · [Documentation Needs](#documentation-needs)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="pitia" name="PITIA">
Principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and association dues — the full housing
payment. DSCR and DTI checks in NEXUS run against PITIA, not just the P&amp;I
payment, because that is the number the ratio really has to cover.
[Used in: Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="prepayment-penalty" name="Prepayment penalty">
A charge for paying the loan off early, common on investor products. NEXUS
captures it where the borrower&apos;s details call for it and surfaces it as a
difference between programs in the matrix and on program cards — it is
eligibility-neutral but deal-shaping.
[Used in: Lender List](#lender-list) · [Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="reserves" name="Reserves">
Liquid assets the borrower must retain after closing, quoted in months of the
PITIA payment. Every program row shows its reserves requirement (`6 mo`); the
**Lowest Reserves** pill flags the friendliest option on the results page for
borrowers who are asset-thin.
[Used in: Programs](#programs) · [Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>

<GlossaryTerm id="seasoning" name="Seasoning">
Required time an asset, title, or credit event must have been in place before
the loan (e.g. self-employment history, recent foreclosure). When a guideline
states one, NEXUS encodes it as a rule and flags scenarios that walk into it —
recent events surface in **Potential Issues**.
[Used in: Reading Your Results](#reading-results)
</GlossaryTerm>
