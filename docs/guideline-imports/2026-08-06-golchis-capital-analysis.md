## DSCR — Single Asset

### Core rules

- Purpose/occupancy: commercial/business-purpose financing for non-owner-occupied, income-producing residential real estate. Business Purpose Affidavit required. [Policy p.2 §1; p.5 §8]
- Loan amount: $75,000 minimum; $3,000,000 maximum. [Product Matrix p.1]
- Borrower exposure: $10 million and/or 10 properties; higher exposure requires additional review. [Policy p.2 §4]
- Minimum standard FICO: 640. Foreign National uses a separate row with no stated numeric FICO. [Product Matrix p.1]
- Mortgage history: maximum 1x30 in the preceding 12 months. [Product Matrix p.1; Policy p.4 §6]
- Major credit events: no bankruptcy, foreclosure, short sale, or other stated event in preceding 36 months. Chapter 13 must be discharged; see clarification item below. [Product Matrix p.1; Policy p.4 §6]
- First-time homebuyer: ineligible. [Policy p.3 §4]
- First-time investor: allowed on Single Asset with a five-point reduction from matrix LTV. [Policy p.3 §4]
- Experienced investor: owned at least two properties for at least 12 months during the most recent 36 months, with at least one income-producing. [Policy p.3 §4]
- Minimum DSCR: 0.75 for long-term rental; 1.00 for STR; 1.25 for 5–10 units. [Product Matrix p.1; Policy pp.4–5 §§7,10]
- Interest-only: maximum 75% LTV. [Product Matrix p.1]
- CLTV/HCLTV: Not specified in the provided guidelines.
- Maximum note rate: Not specified in the provided guidelines.

### Condo project overlays

- Project concentration: 20% maximum.
- HOA delinquency over 30 days: 15% maximum.
- Sellout: at least 90%.
- Renter concentration: 50% maximum unless documented borrower-control exception applies.
- Single-entity ownership: 25% maximum, subject to stated borrower exception.
- Project must be 100% complete.
- Commercial component: 40% maximum of square footage.
- Non-association-fee income: 20% maximum of association income.
- FNMA 1076 or FHLMC full questionnaire required. [Policy p.7 §17]

### Citizenship and residency

- U.S. citizens and Permanent Resident Aliens: eligible without a stated special LTV cap. [Policy p.2 §4]
- Non-Permanent Resident Aliens: E, G, H, L, O, P, or TN visa; established U.S. credit; valid SSN; standard tradelines; maximum 75% LTV. [Product Matrix p.1 restriction 5; Policy pp.2–3 §4]
- EAD-only: ineligible. [Policy p.3 §4]
- Foreign National: eligible with passport and qualifying visa/Canadian/Visa Waiver/Mexican alternatives. General maximum 65% LTV; matrix controls where lower. [Product Matrix p.1; Policy §§4,6]
- Foreign National No-FICO: allowed when no SSN; U.S. credit report and standard tradeline minimum are waived. No-FICO Allowed = YES. [Policy p.4 §6]
- Diplomatic immunity: ineligible. [Policy pp.2–3 §4]
- ITIN: no separate ITIN borrower class is documented. Do not infer ITIN eligibility.

### Credit/tradelines

- Score: lower of two or middle of three for each borrower/guarantor; loan score is the lowest qualifying score among borrowers/guarantors.
- Tradelines: three reporting for 12 months or two reporting for 24 months.
- Refinance of delinquent loan: prohibited.
- Outstanding lien or unpaid judgment over $5,000: prohibited. [Policy p.4 §6]

### Cash-out, refinance, and seasoning

- Cash-out maximum: $1,000,000; unlimited at ≤50% LTV. [Product Matrix p.1]
- Cash-out before three months ownership: not allowed.
- Three to six months ownership: use lower of cost basis or value for LTV.
- Six months or more: no additional value restriction stated.
- Cash-out definition: net proceeds exceeding lesser of 2% of loan amount or $2,000.
- Delayed financing: free-and-clear property acquired less than six months; arm’s-length cash acquisition; no mortgage used; AIV required; new loan cannot exceed cost basis including improvements.
- Rate/term: refinance that is not cash-out; AIV required; delinquent-loan refinance prohibited. [Policy p.2 §3]

### DSCR and rent rules

Formula: Gross Rent ÷ PITIA. For IO loans, initial IO payment is used in place of principal and interest. [Policy p.4 §7]

- Occupied, valid lease: lower of in-place lease or market rent.
- Occupied STR: lower of trailing-12-month net income statement or AirDNA market rent.
- Unoccupied purchase: long-term market rent.
- Unoccupied refinance: 90% of long-term market rent.
- Month-to-month: in-place lease with two months receipt proof, or long-term market rent.
- Lease beginning within 30 days: lease plus verified security deposit, or long-term market rent.
- Third-party rent vendor: 90% of vendor rent; DSCR ≥1.15 after haircut. [Policy §§7,10,13]

STR restrictions: 1–4 units; refinance only; DSCR ≥1.00; trailing-12-month occupancy ≥50%; active platform listing; trailing-12-month income supported by deposits/platform statements; future bookings excluded; single-room STR and appraisal-addendum STR rent ineligible. [Policy p.5 §10]

### Reserves and funds

- Standard: 3 months PITIA.
- Loan >$1.5M: 6 months PITIA.
- DSCR <1.00: 6 months PITIA.
- Foreign National: 12 months PITIA held in U.S. institution for at least 60 days.
- Cash-out proceeds may satisfy reserves.
- Retirement funds: 60% with access evidence; liquidation if used to close.
- Gifts: up to 50% of down payment and/or reserves.
- HELOCs, lines of credit, and debt are not liquidity.
- Business assets require proof of ownership. [Policy pp.3,5 §§4,11]

### Products and prepayment penalties

- 30-year fixed and 30-year fixed IO.
- 5/6 ARM and IO: 2/2/5 caps, 6.00% margin.
- 7/6 ARM and IO: 5/2/5 caps, 6.00% margin.
- IO period: 120 months; remaining amortization 240 months.
- ARM floor: start rate.
- Index and maximum note rate: Not specified.
- General prepayment term/structure: Not specified. New Jersey entity overlay applies if a non-corporate borrowing entity has a prepayment penalty. [Product Matrix; Policy §§2,4]

### Crossed-specific rules

- DSCR: aggregate ≥1.15.
- Experienced investors only; first-time investor ineligible.
- Three to ten properties, all in same state.
- Minimum as-is value: $100,000/property; for 2+ units, $60,000/door.
- Occupancy: at least 90% under third-party Eligible Tenant leases.
- Borrower, owners, and immediate family cannot occupy collateral for qualification.
- Partial release: at least 120% of allocated loan amount.
- Allocation proportional to appraised values.
- Substitutions not allowed.
- Program-wide borrower, credit event, reserve, product, appraisal, geography, and collateral rules apply unless specifically modified. [Policy §§5,7,9]
