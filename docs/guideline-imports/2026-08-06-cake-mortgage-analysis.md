# NON-QM Nexus Guideline Analysis — Cake Mortgage

- **Lender:** Cake Mortgage Corp. — Wholesale Lending Division
- **Document:** Non-QM Loan Eligibility Guidelines, Version 4.0
- **Effective date:** April 1, 2026
- **Date reviewed:** August 6, 2026
- **Uploaded file:** cf475931-d28d-427c-9c81-6db930a5c884.pdf
- **Document completeness:** The uploaded PDF contains the complete 43-page narrative guide: contents, eligibility, visa matrix, title/vesting, transactions, credit, Full Doc, Alternative Documentation, liabilities, assets/reserves, collateral, condominium requirements, title and miscellaneous overlays. It does **not** contain the separate current Cake program/credit matrix repeatedly referenced for exact FICO, LTV/CLTV, loan amount, mortgage-rating, adverse-credit seasoning, reserve and declining-market tiers.

## Executive conclusion

Cake offers a materially broader Non-QM shelf than the single DSCR record previously visible in NON-QM Nexus. Version 4.0 expressly documents Full Documentation and seven alternative qualification methods: Bank Statement, P&L Only, P&L with Bank Statements, 1099, WVOE Only, ATR-in-Full (AiF), and Asset Utilization. The guide also documents eligible ITIN and non-permanent-resident borrower classes, but it does not establish a separate stand-alone ITIN product name or a Foreign National program. Existing Cake DSCR records derived from separate sources were preserved and not overwritten by this general guide.

Because the separate current matrix was not supplied, exact numeric tiers are displayed as **Current matrix required** rather than as zero, no minimum, or an invented maximum. These programs can now surface as manual-review candidates based on their confirmed documentation, occupancy, property, citizenship and qualitative rules.

## Cross-program rules

### Transactions
- Purchase, rate/term refinance and cash-out refinance are addressed in §§3.1-3.3.
- Rate/term incidental cash is the greater of 1% of the new loan or $2,000.
- Closed-end subordinate financing not used for acquisition generally requires 12 months seasoning for rate/term treatment; HELOC requires 12 months plus less than $2,000 in prior-12-month draws.
- Cash-out requires 90 days title seasoning, except inheritance/divorce-related exceptions. A free-and-clear property is cash-out.
- Delayed financing within 90 days is treated as cash-out; reimbursement cannot exceed the documented initial investment and applicable cash-out LTV.
- Secondary financing is permitted if subordinated; private-party seconds are not. HELOC CLTV uses the full line unless past the draw period.
- Investment-property prepayment penalties may run 1-5 years where lawful (§3.10).

### Citizenship and residency
- U.S. citizens and permanent residents are eligible (§§2.1.1-2.1.2).
- Non-permanent residents require current employment-authorized visa/EAD documentation; expired documents require renewal/extension evidence (§2.1.3).
- Cake’s visa matrix is unusually broad and includes H-1B/H-4, L-1, E-1/E-2/E-3, O, R-1, TN, G/NATO, DACA, TPS, asylee, refugee, VAWA and other listed classifications (§2.2).
- ITIN borrowers require a valid ITIN card or IRS letter, unexpired government photo identification and a U.S. credit report. USCIS residency documentation is expressly waived for ITIN borrowers (§2.1.4).
- Foreign National eligibility is **Not specified in the provided Version 4.0 general guideline**. A non-permanent resident is not the same as a Foreign National.

### Credit
- A tri-merged report is required for each individual borrower/guarantor (§4).
- Normally at least two scores are required; no-FICO/single-FICO exists only where the missing program matrix permits it (§4.2).
- Qualifying score is lower of two/middle of three for the borrower with the highest qualifying income.
- Standard tradelines: 3 reporting for 12 months with recent activity, or 2 for 24 months with recent activity. Requirements are deemed met if the primary wage earner has three scores (§4.3).
- Alternative thin-file paths are documented, including eight tradelines with mortgage/rent history or substitution with alternative tradelines.
- A 12-month housing history is reviewed. Up to six 30-day occurrences within a rolling period count as one event; lates above 30 days each count separately. Exact permitted events require the matrix (§§4.6-4.7).
- Bankruptcy, foreclosure, short-sale, deed-in-lieu and modification seasoning are **Not specified in the provided narrative guideline**; §4.8 defers them to the matrix.
- Active forbearance is ineligible. CCCS may be acceptable after 12 months timely plan payments with authorization to seek new financing.

### Assets, reserves, gifts and concessions
- Exact reserve months are **Not specified**; §8.7 refers to the matrix.
- Rate/term reserves are waived when payment savings is at least 10%, unless DTI exceeds 50%.
- Cash-out proceeds and gift funds may satisfy reserves; 1031 proceeds may not.
- Gifts may be used for down payment, closing costs, reserves and debt payoff. Investment borrowers must demonstrate 10% own funds, although those funds need not be contributed (§8.4).
- Seller concessions: owner-occupied ≤80% LTV up to 9%; owner-occupied >80% up to 6%; non-owner-occupied up to 6% (§8.8).

### Property
- Eligible: detached/attached SFR, 2-4 units, condo, non-warrantable condo, condotel, manufactured, mixed use under FNMA, modular, leasehold, ADU and properties up to 20 acres (§9.4).
- Second homes are limited to SFR, condo, PUD and townhouse; no multi-unit second homes (§2.8.6).
- Ineligible: co-op, agricultural, barndominium, boarding/group/assisted-living, log, houseboat, dome, commercial, under-construction, <500 sq. ft., non-year-round, inaccessible and rural >20 acres (§9.5).
- Non-warrantable condo and condotel criteria are detailed in §9.11.2; exact LTV restrictions require the matrix.

## Program-by-program analysis

## 1. Full Documentation

**Documentation:** Wage earners: 30 days paystubs plus one or two years W-2s/transcripts. Self-employed: one or two years signed personal/business returns with proof of filing. 4506-C is required. §§5.1-5.4.

**Employment:** Two-year history. A >60-day gap requires explanation and at least six months with the current employer. Part-time requires 12 months. Variable income generally requires two years; less than one year is ineligible. §§5, 5.3.2, 5.7.

**DTI/residual:** Maximum DTI 50% or as allowed by the matrix (§7.2). Consumer-purpose loans require $1,500 residual; business-purpose loans are exempt (§5.6).

**Occupancy/property/citizenship:** Primary, second home and investment under general rules; citizens, permanent residents, eligible non-permanent residents and ITIN borrowers under §2. Exact matrix combinations require confirmation.

**FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves/mortgage lates/adverse credit:** Not specified in the provided narrative guideline; current matrix required.

## 2. Bank Statement — 12/24 Month

**Statements:** 12 or 24 consecutive months. Business statements cannot be co-mingled across accounts to complete the period. Most recent statement within 60 days. §§6.1.1-6.1.2.

**Self-employment:** At least 25% ownership; same business at least one year and two years in the same line of business.

**Business statements:** 50% standard expense ratio. A CPA, EA, CTEC preparer, PTIN holder or Tax Attorney may support a different ratio, minimum 10%. Below-50% requires a business-expense letter or P&L.

**Personal statements:** 12/24 months plus two recent business statements. With those statements, 100% of personal deposits may be considered. If no business account exists, personal statements may be used with a 10% expense factor.

**NSF:** At ≥80% LTV, maximum 12 occurrences in 12 months and no more than 3 in the latest 3 months. Below 80%, no NSF restriction. The earlier catalog note saying all NSFs were prohibited was incorrect and has been superseded (§6.1.3).

**Tax returns:** Not required. 4506-C not required (§6.1.1).

**Rental/STR supplement:** 100% verified monthly rental income may supplement. STR uses a 12-month platform history plus active-listing screenshot (§6.2).

**FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves/mortgage lates/adverse credit:** Not specified; current matrix required.

## 3. P&L Statement Only — 12/24 Month

**Borrower:** Self-employed, minimum 25% ownership; same business at least one year and two years in same line (§6.3).

**Income document:** A 12- or 24-month P&L prepared by CPA, EA, CTEC preparer, PTIN holder or Tax Attorney. End date within 60 days of application and document no older than 120 days at closing.

**Calculation:** Net income ÷ months covered × ownership percentage. Depreciation, depletion and amortization may be added back. Two separate business P&Ls are allowed.

**Tax returns:** The P&L itself is the income document. Borrower tax returns and 4506-C are not required. A preparer’s attestation under §6.10.2 confirms filing/review/business existence only; it does not convert the program into tax-return qualification.

**FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves:** Not specified; current matrix required.

## 4. P&L with Bank Statements — 12/24 Month

This is a distinct supported-P&L method under §6.4.

- 12/24-month P&L from licensed preparer.
- Three months bank statements.
- Deposits may not be more than 25% below P&L revenue; more statements may be added until tolerance is met.
- NSF events and overdrafts are not permitted for this option.
- Tax returns and 4506-C are not required.
- Exact FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves require the current matrix.

## 5. 1099 Only — 1/2 Year

- Two-year 1099 history, or one year after W-2-to-1099 conversion in the same line of work.
- One or two years 1099s/transcripts.
- 90% net margin / 10% expense factor.
- Two recent bank statements must support deposits.
- YTD earnings within 10% of or above prior-year earnings.
- Supplemental W-2/Social Security may be included if not dominant (§6.5).
- Exact FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves require the current matrix.

## 6. WVOE Only

- Primary residence only.
- Two years with same employer.
- FNMA Form 1005 completed by HR, payroll or company officer.
- Family-employed borrowers ineligible.
- Paystubs, W-2s, tax returns and 4506-C not required.
- FTHB eligible subject to matrix.
- Minimum 10% borrower contribution.
- Bonus/commission/overtime allowed if base pay is majority and variable income is not declining.
- No other active-employment income; passive rental income may supplement.
- VVOE within 3 days of closing (§6.6).
- Exact FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves require the matrix.

## 7. ATR-in-Full (AiF)

A unique Cake program under §6.7.

- Personally held liquid assets sufficient to pay the loan in full satisfy DTI and residual-income requirements.
- Two recent asset statements required.
- Business accounts generally ineligible.
- Underwriter considers minimum balance and depletion.
- Delayed-financing proceeds may qualify if sourced and seasoned for 90 days before the initial cash purchase.
- This differs from Asset Utilization: AiF satisfies ATR with full-payoff liquidity rather than creating monthly income through a divisor.
- Exact FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves require the current matrix.

## 8. Asset Utilization

**Calculation:** Net Qualified Assets ÷ 60 months. Crypto is 50% value ÷ 84 months (§6.8.1).

**Haircuts:**
- 100% checking, savings, money market, CDs and net cash-value life insurance.
- 70% publicly traded stocks/bonds/mutual funds.
- Retirement: 70% at age 59½+, 50% below 59½.
- Bitcoin/Ethereum: 50%, approved exchange only; no personal/cold wallets.
- Trust assets eligible.

**Ineligible:** Real-estate equity, private/restricted/non-vested stock, assets producing income already counted, business-held assets.

**Verification:** Three monthly statements or quarterly statement; 30-day seasoning.

**Gifts:** Permitted except above 80% LTV/CLTV.

**FICO/LTV/loan amount/reserves:** Not specified; current matrix required.

## 9. DSCR and Foreign National

Version 4.0 mentions DSCR for entity vesting and business-purpose investment loans but contains no complete DSCR income/ratio section or numeric DSCR matrix. Existing Cake DSCR 1-4 and DSCR Foreign National records originate from separate sources and were preserved. No DSCR ratio, STR method, vacancy rule, first-time-investor rule or numeric DSCR grid was imported from this PDF.

Foreign National eligibility is not established by Version 4.0. The extensive visa matrix applies to non-permanent residents legally present in the U.S.; it must not be treated as Foreign National eligibility.

## Strongest selling points

1. Very broad alternative-documentation shelf.
2. One-year self-employment path for Bank Statement and P&L.
3. P&L Only with no borrower tax returns.
4. WVOE requiring no paystubs, W-2s, returns or 4506-C.
5. Unique AiF full-payoff-liquidity option.
6. Asset Utilization with a 60-month divisor and limited Bitcoin/Ethereum treatment.
7. Broad visa/EAD matrix including DACA, TPS, refugee/asylee and G/NATO categories.
8. Broad property coverage including manufactured homes, condotels and non-warrantable condos, subject to matrix overlays.

## Most important restrictions

1. Exact numeric program eligibility cannot be finalized without the current matrix.
2. WVOE is primary-only and requires 10% borrower contribution.
3. P&L-with-statements prohibits NSF/overdraft events.
4. Bank Statement requires 25% ownership, one year in business and two years in the same line.
5. Business entities may borrow only under DSCR; non-DSCR programs use natural-person/trust vesting rules.
6. Second homes cannot be multi-unit or rental properties.
7. Active forbearance is ineligible.
8. Many specialty/unique properties are excluded despite otherwise broad collateral eligibility.

## Conflicts and clarification items

1. Obtain Cake’s current program/credit matrix for FICO, LTV/CLTV, loan amounts, reserves, mortgage-late tiers, adverse-credit seasoning and declining-market caps.
2. Confirm which non-DSCR programs extend to ITIN borrowers and any occupancy/LTV reductions; the general eligibility section is broad but the absent matrix may narrow it.
3. Confirm no-FICO/single-FICO availability by program.
4. Confirm whether Full Doc “one or two years” depends on a named matrix tier.
5. Resolve the guide’s title-language tension: inter-vivos revocable trust is listed as eligible vesting, while trusts are listed under ineligible borrowers. The safe reading is trust vesting may be eligible but a trust cannot be the borrowing applicant; confirm operationally.
6. Obtain a separate current DSCR guide/matrix before changing existing DSCR numeric records.
