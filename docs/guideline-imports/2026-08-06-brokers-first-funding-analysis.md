### Major changes detected
- BFF’s existing four original records were pending-review marketing stubs. The supplied official guidelines confirm program mechanics and many overlays.
- DSCR Closed-End Second is now a separate, premier standalone-second product—not merged into first-lien DSCR.
- WVOE has explicit 680 FICO, $1.5M max, 80% purchase/rate-term and 70% cash-out parameters.
- Asset Utilization has exact loan-balance tiers, 60-month divisor, asset haircuts, and 45% DTI.
- Bank Statement has detailed personal/business/co-mingled calculations, NSF tolerances, 50% standard expense factor and third-party expense-factor methods.
- P&L Only explicitly requires no tax returns or 4506-C; the P&L is the income document. The preparer’s tax-filing attestation does not make this tax-return documentation.
- Main DSCR guide now addresses first-time investors, first-time owner/investors, STRs, vacant properties, Non-Permanent Residents, and Foreign Nationals.
- 5-8 / mixed-use DSCR is experienced-investor only with no STR income, no gifts, two-acre cap and commercial-space limits.

- DSCR STR: purchase lesser of 75% or matrix; refinance lesser of 70% or matrix.
- NPRE: Full/Expanded max 80%; DSCR max 75%.

### Citizenship
- U.S. citizens and permanent residents eligible under normal program scope.
- Non-Permanent Residents: 2 years U.S. residence and employment, valid SSN, U.S. credit and EAD/eligible visa. Accepted visas listed in §4.1.3: E-1, E-2, E-3, G-1 through G-5, H-1B, L-1A, L-1B, O-1, R-1. Asylees and DACA are addressed. Full/Expanded max 80%; DSCR investment max 75%.
- ITIN: owner-occupied only under select ITIN products; valid ITIN and two-year consistent ITIN payment history; DACA requires U.S. driver’s license and EAD. No-FICO ITIN is **not established**.
- Foreign National: separate investment-only DSCR program. Foreign/alternative credit can be used; passport/legal entry, foreign residence, ACH and OFAC screening required. Not eligible for DSCR 5-8, mixed use, or cross collateral.
- CES: Foreign Nationals and borrowers without a valid SSN are ineligible under §5.4.2.

### Property types
- General shelf: SFR, attached SFR, 2-4 unit, condo, condotel, non-warrantable condo, leasehold, 5-8 residential and 2-8 mixed use where program-specific.
- Main guide excludes manufactured/mobile, modular, log, co-op, rural, agricultural, boarding/bed-and-breakfast and various unique/non-residential properties.
- DSCR 5-8/mixed use: residential 5-8 or mixed-use 2-8; commercial <=49.99%; retail/office/restaurant only; no vacant commercial space; maximum two acres.
- CES is narrower: SFR/PUD/townhome/rowhome/modular, 2-4 units and warrantable condos. CES excludes rural, condotel, non-warrantable condo, manufactured/mobile, log, unique, co-op and mixed-use; maximum 10 acres.

### Mortgage lates / housing
- General guide: program matrix controls mortgage-late tiers; all reported mortgage history is evaluated. Current accounts are required at application.
- DSCR first-time owner+investor: if reported, no mortgage/rent late in 24 months.
- CES: satisfactory housing history; purchase requires 12 months, standalone cash-out requires current senior lien history; piggyback may follow first-lien approval. DSCR CES requires satisfactory 12-month primary and subject history. Exact allowed 30-day-late count remains **not specified in the provided CES guide** because the matrix is absent.

### Cash-out / rate-term / seasoning
- General cash-out: six months ownership to use current appraised value; less than six months uses lower purchase cost plus documented improvements/current value, with inheritance/divorce exceptions. Prior cash-out within six months is ineligible. Free-and-clear is cash-out. Cash-out may satisfy reserves except DSCR 5-8/mixed use.
- General rate-term: refinance acquisition liens and seasoned subordinate financing; closed-end/HELOC subordinate debt generally requires 12 months to qualify as rate-term.
- CES: standalone cash-out or purchase piggyback generally; DSCR CES is standalone cash-out only. CES requires six months ownership. Prior cash-out within six months caps CLTV at 80%. Property listed in prior six months is ineligible.

### Major credit events
- General minimum seasoning: 12 months for bankruptcy, foreclosure, short sale/DIL, but program matrices may require longer. Recent forbearance/modification within 12 months is ineligible for DSCR/5-8/mixed use.
- CES: no foreclosure action, short sale/DIL or bankruptcy in prior 48 months; no multiple credit/housing events in prior seven years.

### Reserves
- General matrices control reserves. A rate-term reserve waiver can apply to 1-4 Full/Expanded/DSCR with >=10% P&I reduction, housing <=1x30x12 and DTI <=50%.
- First-time owner+investor DSCR requires 12 months PITIA reserves.
- Foreign National minimum six months noted in the guide.
- CES reserve months, including DSCR CES, are **not specified in the provided CES guide**; current matrix required.

### DSCR
- Main 1-4 DSCR: investment/business purpose; gross rents / PITIA (or ITIA for IO). Standard minimum depends on matrix; product page advertises no-ratio. Loan amount below $150K requires 1.25 DSCR. STR purchase/refinance allowed with 20% minimum expense factor and 75%/70% caps before matrix. First-time-investor overlays are program-specific.
- 5-8/mixed use: minimum 1.00, experienced investor only, no STR, no gifts.
- Closed-End Second: minimum 1.00, experienced investor, standalone cash-out only, minimum one-year PPP where lawful, note-rate PITIA, 1007/STR analysis, current matrix required for DSCR CES CLTV/FICO/loan amount/reserves.

### Prepayment penalties
- Main investment programs: one to five years where legal; fixed 5% or six-month-interest structures, but 5-8/mixed-use excludes the six-month-interest structure.
- DSCR CES: minimum one year where allowed by state.

### Maximum rate
**Not specified in the provided guidelines.**

## ITEMS REQUIRING HUMAN REVIEW

1. **Missing current product matrices.** Both guides explicitly state that exact LTV/FICO/DTI/reserve/loan-limit tiers come from the current matrices. Headline figures are not treated as universally available.
2. **P&L Only LTV wording.** General guide §8.8 includes a PTIN tier at 75% primary/70% second-home-investment and separately references restrictions for an >80%-85% tier, while BFF’s product page advertises an 80% maximum. The current P&L/Expanded matrix is needed.
3. **DSCR CES numeric tiers.** $850K, 90% CLTV and 660 are official overall CES product ceilings, but the uploaded CES guideline does not prove that all three apply to the DSCR CES subset.
4. **DSCR 1-4 no-ratio leverage.** The official BFF product page confirms no-ratio exists, but the current matrix must determine the permissible no-ratio LTV/FICO/loan tiers.
5. **Mixed-use representation.** The platform currently maps the dedicated 5-8/mixed-use program through `5_8_unit`; an explicit mixed-use enum would improve future precision.
